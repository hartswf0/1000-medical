Production record

Creative thesis

The ladder can only see what is painted on the card: titles, charts, headcount. Odie's craft has no paint on it, so the film draws it as the one thing cardboard can't hold: a thin line of light from his head to the work. The film is built toward a single moment in the orchard, when his father closes a pair of calipers "without a hair of light" and recognizes the same invisible tolerance in his son. Pride moves off the title and onto the hand.

Defining passage

Chapter 13, the orchard. Long holds, the father keeps grafting, the trees get named, then the bugs get named as grafts on "the committee" tree. The calipers close. The TEAM LEAD? tag is cut loose and the hand lands on the shoulder.

Formal choices

  • Brad-jointed puppets built from your card sheet (torso 57 cm, thigh 47, shin 43; 10 cm = 60 px). Odie's limbs and heads are your painted cardboard pieces.
  • Paper-theatre staging: layered cards, one lamp, real cast shadows. Everything moves on pivots; nothing morphs.
  • Status is a paper tag on a string. The ladder hangs tags; the father cuts one.
  • Sound is synthesized live: room tone, card slides, brad clicks, a five-note motif that only resolves in the orchard.

Quality references

  • Lotte Reiniger, The Adventures of Prince Achmed (1926): acting carried entirely by joint timing and silhouette.
  • Yuri Norstein, Tale of Tales (1979): layered cut-out depth, and holds that carry thought.
  • Terry Gilliam's Monty Python cut-outs: comic timing of snaps against stillness, for the office and the Cyclops.
  • Homer, Odyssey 24 (Laertes's orchard): recognition through a shared practice rather than a sign.

Limitation: no agent in this production could play the reference films. They are named as targets. Review used still frames from this render, not the films.

Independent review

Critic 1 (whole film, stills): reference stronger. Primary gap: the father's hand never touched the son, because they stood on different ground lines at different scales. The tag fell before the touch with no one cutting it. The father's brow never changed. Protected: the calipers insert, the trees and bugs named as tags, the warming light, the office satire frames.

Repair. Same ground line and scale for both. The father walks in and his forearm lands on the shoulder and holds. The father's brow and mouth now change at "proud". He cuts the tag with his grafting knife after the touch. The bug tags moved out from behind him, and the tree captions are staggered.

Critic 2 (fresh, orchard only): touch and cut order met. Ground line not met. Cause: the drawn puppets' legs were about 45 px shorter than Odie's sprite legs, film-wide. Fixed in the rig, so every scene is corrected. Odie now reaches back to his father's arm.

Status: production milestone, not a final film. Every judgement came from stills. No reviewer watched playback timing or heard the sound.
